The opportunity

Your screens have the opportunity to say something different.

Digital signage makes it possible to change what shoppers see throughout the day. Yet keeping content fresh, relevant and consistent with a brand takes time and creative effort.

SocialsEasy helps address that challenge by generating short, brand-specific messages for retail signage, giving storefront screens more ways to capture attention and spark interest.

What SocialsEasy does

More than a fixed rotation of messages.

SocialsEasy generates signage messages using a retailer's brand, products, positioning and communication objectives as context.

Rather than relying on a static set of creatives, messages can be continuously refreshed to introduce variety and keep storefront communication current.

The focus is on the message itself: what a shopper can notice, read and connect with in the few seconds they pass a screen.

How it works

Configure once. Generate and refresh continuously.

  • Brand context. Establish the brand voice, product context, positioning and communication objectives.
  • Creative direction. Establish a direction for messaging rather than treating each refresh as an isolated copywriting exercise.
  • Message generation. Generate concise messages suited to storefront viewing and the limited attention available to passing shoppers.
  • Evaluation. Assess messages for brand relevance, creative alignment, distinctiveness and length.
  • Continuous refresh. Generate and deliver new messages at configured intervals, without requiring the retailer to create each message manually.

Built for digital signage

Content designed around the screen and its context.

Signage content has different demands from print advertising, social media or long-form marketing copy. Messages need to work within screen layouts, viewing distances, dwell times and playback schedules.

  • Short-form messaging. Messages designed for quick reading, limited screen space and brief viewing windows.
  • Display-aware delivery. Concise text content suited to the intended signage layout, orientation and screen environment.
  • Refresh cadence. Content generation configured around the frequency at which messages need to change.
  • Existing signage infrastructure. Focused on the content layer, complementing existing displays, media players and signage management systems rather than replacing them.
